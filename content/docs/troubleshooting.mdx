Something does not work while ClearURLs is turned on?
This page helps you find out **whether ClearURLs is the cause** and **what you can do about it**,
step by step, without any technical knowledge.

<Callout type="info" title="The two most important things first">

1. If a website does not work, [turn the filter off and reload the page](#first-check). In most cases this immediately tells you whether ClearURLs is involved.
2. If you want to report a problem, please read [How to report a buggy website](/reporting). A good report helps us fix the problem much faster.

</Callout>
---

## First check: is ClearURLs causing the problem? [#first-check]

ClearURLs removes tracking fields from addresses (URLs). Very rarely a website needs one of
these fields and stops working correctly without it. This is how you find out. It takes less than
a minute:

1. Open the website that has the problem.
2. Click the **ClearURLs icon** in your browser toolbar (the orange logo next to the address bar).
3. In the small window that opens, find the section **Configs** and turn the switch
   **Filter** off.
4. **Reload the website** (press the <kbd>f5</kbd> key or click the reload button).
5. Try again what did not work before.

**What does the result mean?**

- **The website now works:** ClearURLs is involved in the problem.
  Turn the **Filter** switch on again and continue reading in
  [Known problems and fixes](#known-problems) below. For many websites there is already a solution.
- **The website still does not work:** ClearURLs is probably *not* the cause.
  Please also check other extensions (for example ad blockers) or the website itself.
  Do not forget to turn the **Filter** switch back on.

<Callout type="info" title="Turning the filter off does not uninstall anything">

The **Filter** switch only pauses the cleaning. Your settings and the rules stay in place.
You can turn it back on at any time with the same switch.

</Callout>
If you want to know what exactly ClearURLs changes on a specific address, you can also use the
built-in **Cleaning Tool**: click the ClearURLs icon and choose the **wrench-and-screwdriver icon**
(screwdriver and wrench crossed), paste the address there and press **Clean URLs**.
This shows you the cleaned address without visiting the website.

## Known problems and fixes [#known-problems]

The following problems have been **reported by users**. The list shows the current state of our
knowledge. When a problem is fixed in the rules or in the add-on, it disappears from here.
Behind every entry you find a link to the public bug report with all technical details.

<details className="fd-details" data-oldid="google-ai-mode-and-ai-overviews-show-error-400-or-nothing-at-all">
<summary>Google: AI Mode and AI Overviews show error 400 or nothing at all</summary>

**What happens:** When you use Google's *AI Mode*, or when a search would show an
*AI Overview*, you instead get an error page ("400. That's an error.") or the answer
simply does not appear.

**Why:** Google checks parts of the address very strictly. A field that ClearURLs removes
is expected by the AI feature, so Google refuses the request.

**What you can do:**

- Deactivate the **Filter** switch (see [above](#first-check)) while you use the AI search, or
- wait for a rules update. The problem is known and being worked on. The add-on updates
  its rules automatically.

**Bug reports:** [Addon #495](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/495),
[Addon #508](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/508),
[Addon #522](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/522),
[Rules #182](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Rules/issues/182)

</details>
<details className="fd-details" data-oldid="google-docs-sheets-slides-dialogs-search-or-filters-do-not-open">
<summary>Google Docs / Sheets / Slides: dialogs, search or filters do not open</summary>

**What happens:** Inside Google Docs, Sheets or Slides some functions stop reacting,
for example the document search (<kbd>ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>f</kbd>), inserting a link, filters or data
validation dialogs.

**Why:** These Google apps change the address *inside* the page (without reloading it) to open
dialogs. The ClearURLs option that protects you against tracking through this technique
unfortunately also confuses these Google apps.

**What you can do (recommended fix):**

1. Click the ClearURLs icon and open the **settings** (the gear symbol).
2. Find the option **Prevent tracking injection over history API** and turn it off.
3. Click **Save & reload addon**.
4. Reload the Google document. If it still fails, additionally clear your browser cache once
   (<kbd>ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>shift</kbd> + <kbd>del</kbd>, choose "Cached images and files").

Your protection stays almost the same. Only this one technique is no longer blocked.
If even that does not help, turn the **Filter** switch off while you work in Google Docs.

**Bug reports:** [Addon #476](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/476),
[Addon #417](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/417),
[Addon #311](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/311)

</details>
<details className="fd-details" data-oldid="google-search-results-reload-again-and-again-in-a-loop">
<summary>Google: search results reload again and again in a loop</summary>

**What happens:** A page reloads many times in a row. You can watch the address change
between `+` and `%20`, and sometimes the search stops completely.
On some websites (for example Google Trends) this can even make your IP address look
suspicious to firewalls.

**Why:** Two different ways of writing a space in an address (`+` and `%20`) are converted
back and forth between the website and ClearURLs.

**What you can do:**

- Turn the **Filter** switch off for searches on the affected website.
- Make sure you have the newest add-on version and let the rules update
  (open the **settings** and click **Save & reload addon** once. This also downloads
  the newest rules).

**Bug reports:** [Addon #308](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/308),
[Addon #430](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/430),
[Addon #219](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/219)

</details>
<details className="fd-details" data-oldid="login-does-not-work-google-sign-in-claudeai-banks-">
<summary>Login does not work (Google sign-in, Claude.ai, banks, ...)</summary>

**What happens:** You cannot sign in on a website while ClearURLs is active.
Typical cases:

- **Sign in with Google** buttons that fail or loop, including tools such as the
  `gcloud` command-line program.
- **Claude.ai sign-in links** ("magic links") from e-mails are rejected, because the end
  of the sign-in code is removed from the address.
- Some **banks and payment pages** refuse to continue.

**Why:** Sign-in systems put secret one-time codes into the address. In rare cases a rule
mistakes such a code for a tracking field and removes it.

**What you can do:**

- Turn the **Filter** switch off **before** you click the sign-in link, sign in,
  then turn it back on. Most websites only need the special address *during* sign-in.
- For Claude.ai: copy the full link from the e-mail, turn the filter off, and only then
  open the link.

**Bug reports:** [Addon #510](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/510),
[Addon #258](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/258),
[Addon #445](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/445),
[Addon #320](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/320)

</details>
<details className="fd-details" data-oldid="amazon-sorting-wish-lists-or-the-shopping-cart-do-not-work">
<summary>Amazon: sorting, wish lists or the shopping cart do not work</summary>

**What happens:** On Amazon the *sort results* dropdown, editing a *wish list*, or sometimes
the *shopping cart* does not react when ClearURLs is active.

**Why:** Amazon uses some address fields for two things at once: tracking *and* shop functions.
We maintain a list of Amazon fields that must **not** be removed, but Amazon changes its
website often.

**What you can do:**

- Make sure your rules are up to date: open the ClearURLs **settings** and click
  **Save & reload addon** once.
- If it still fails, turn the **Filter** switch off while you shop.
- You can read which fields we deliberately keep (and why) on the page
  [Excluded Fields](/further_readings/excluded_fields).

**Bug reports:** [Rules #227](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Rules/issues/227),
[Rules #295](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Rules/issues/295),
[Addon #392](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/392),
[Addon #518](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/518)

</details>
<details className="fd-details" data-oldid="reddit-bing-linkedin-icloud-or-other-well-known-sites-misbehave">
<summary>Reddit, Bing, LinkedIn, iCloud or other well-known sites misbehave</summary>

**What happens:** Examples reported by users: Reddit search
([Addon #505](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/505)), Bing autosuggest
([Addon #447](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/447)),
LinkedIn jobs ([Addon #512](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/512)),
iCloud file uploads ([Rules #315](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Rules/issues/315)),
bol.com payments ([Rules #283](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Rules/issues/283)).

**What you can do:**

- First update the rules: ClearURLs **settings**, then **Save & reload addon**.
- Then try the **Filter** switch as described [above](#first-check).
- If the problem stays, please [report it](/reporting). Such reports are exactly
  how the rules get better.

</details>
<details className="fd-details" data-oldid="the-browser-is-suddenly-very-slow-or-uses-a-lot-of-cpu-on-some-pages">
<summary>The browser is suddenly very slow or uses a lot of CPU on some pages</summary>

**What happens:** On a few pages (reported for example for github.com/search and some
Wikipedia pages) one processor core goes to 100% and the tab stutters.

**What you can do:**

- Turn the **Filter** switch off on the affected page.
- Or open the ClearURLs settings, turn **Prevent tracking injection over history API**
  off and click **Save & reload addon**.

**Bug reports:** [Addon #454](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/454),
[Addon #506](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/506),
[Addon #185](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/185)

</details>

## "Chrome, Edge or Brave disabled ClearURLs": the Manifest V2 situation [#mv2]

<Callout type="warn" title="What is happening?">

Google is gradually removing the technology (called "Manifest V2") that the current
ClearURLs version is built on from Chrome and Chrome-based browsers.
As a result, some browsers, for example **Brave** and newer **Chrome** versions,
disable the add-on automatically with a message like
*"This extension was turned off because it's no longer supported"*.

</Callout>
**What this means for you:**

- **Firefox:** nothing changes. ClearURLs works and remains fully supported there.
  Firefox has committed to keep Manifest V2 extensions working.
- **Chrome / Brave and other Chrome-based browsers:** at the moment ClearURLs can be
  disabled automatically by the browser. An update of the add-on for the new technology
  (Manifest V3) is not finished yet.
- **Microsoft Edge**: the same end is expected here as well. Microsoft builds Edge on
  Chromium and has announced it will follow Google's Manifest V2 deprecation, so the
  add-on will stop working on Edge in the coming months.

We are sorry about this. It is a browser decision, not something the add-on can override.
You can follow the technical discussion in the bug reports
[Addon #527](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/527) and
[Addon #528](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/528).

## "Can I turn ClearURLs off for just one website?" [#whitelist]

**Short answer:** A real *website whitelist* ("never touch pages on example.com") is the
most requested feature, but it is **not included in the current released version**.
We know how important this is. It is the topic with by far the most feedback
([Addon #93](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Addon/issues/93) with more than a hundred supporters).

**What you can do today instead:**

| Want to ... | How |
|---|---|
| Pause cleaning once to test or log in | Turn the **Filter** switch off (see [First check](#first-check)) and back on afterwards |
| Stop only address changes *inside* a page (e.g. Google Docs) | Turn off **Prevent tracking injection over history API** in the settings |
| Stop ETag header filtering | Turn off the **ETag filtering** option in the settings |
| Stop hyperlink auditing (ping) blocking | Turn off the **Block hyperlink auditing** option in the settings |

<Callout type="info" title="For technically interested users: your own copy of the rules">

Advanced users can point the add-on to their **own copy of the rule catalog** and add an
`exceptions` entry there for a website. You can enter the two addresses in the ClearURLs
**settings page** (the fields *"The url to the data.json file (rules)"* and
*"The url to the rules.hash file (hash)"*), then press **Save & reload addon**.

Please note:

- You have to host both files yourself and keep them up to date. Otherwise you will
  stop receiving rule improvements.
- The hash file content must be written in **lowercase**, otherwise the check fails
  and the rules are rejected.
- The format of `exceptions` is described in [Rule Catalogs](/specs/rules).

</Callout>
